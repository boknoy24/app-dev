# My Favorite TV Series: Lucifer

**Genre:** Urban Fantasy, Crime, Drama  

*Lucifer* follows **Lucifer Morningstar**, the Devil, who abandons Hell to live in Los Angeles. He runs a nightclub called **Lux** and becomes a consultant for the LAPD, helping Detective **Chloe Decker** solve crimes while exploring themes of redemption, identity, and free will.

## Why I Love It
- Tom Ellis gives an incredible performance as Lucifer.  
- The mix of humor, crime, and supernatural elements keeps it exciting.  
- The emotional development of the characters is well done.  

> *"What is it you truly desire?"* – Lucifer Morningstar
